Meet DJ IZA

I'm DJ IZA, a Dominican and Mexican DJ born and raised in Southwest Detroit. I've been DJing since I was 14 years old, and music has always been a huge part of who I am.

My goal is simple: bring high-energy sets, connect with everybody in the room, and create an environment where everyone feels welcome.

To me, music has no status or boundaries. It's all about coming together, enjoying the moment, and sharing great vibes and even better energy.

🎧

Bringing the Energy Everywhere

From nightclubs to special events, I love creating unforgettable experiences through music and energy.

Every crowd is different, and that's what makes DJing exciting. I enjoy mixing different sounds and reading the room to keep the energy going.
