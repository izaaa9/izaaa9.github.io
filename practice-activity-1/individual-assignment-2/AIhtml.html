Is Artificial Intelligence Right for Me?

If you enjoy technology, solving problems,and learning how things work, artificial intelligence could be a great path. This degree allows students to explore how AI can be used in everyday life and different careers.

What You'll Study

Students learn about artificial intelligence, programming, machine learning, and how technology can solve real-world problems. The program focuses on building technical skills and understanding responsible uses of AI.

Supporting Courses

Supporting courses help students strengthen their knowledge in computing, mathematics, and other areas related to artificial intelligence. These classes provide additional skills that can be applied to future careers.
